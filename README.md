# Organic Intelligence Protocol (OIP)

The Organic Intelligence Protocol (OIP) is a research framework designed to examine whether automated decision systems can incorporate demonstrated real-world outcomes, contextual relevance, and resilience rather than relying primarily on formal credentials, documentation, or infrastructure-dependent signals.

The research methodology follows an evidence-first and fail-closed approach in which unsupported constructs, scores, outcomes, and empirical claims are not forced through incomplete evidence chains.

---

## Core Evidence Chain

The OIP research lineage follows:

**Evidence → Measurement → Score → Evaluation → Claim**

The governing principle is:

> Evidence must authorize measurement.  
> Measurement must authorize score.  
> Score must precede evaluation.  
> Evaluation must precede claim.

Where sufficient evidence is not established, the process remains fail-closed.

Missing evidence is not replaced by inference, unsupported proxies, silent imputation, synthetic outcomes, random fallback, or post-hoc selection.
