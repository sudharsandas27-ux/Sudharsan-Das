Organic Intelligence Protocol

The Organic Intelligence Protocol (OIP) is a research-stage methodology for evidence-based construct measurement, dataset qualification, analytical authorization, and evidence-bounded decision systems.

Governing sequence

"Evidence → Measurement → Score → Evaluation → Claim"

Core principles

- No evidence, no operationalization.
- Missing ≠ 0.
- No silent imputation or unsupported proxies.
- No synthetic outcomes presented as empirical findings.
- Unresolved evidence remains unresolved.
- Claims must remain within the available evidence.

v0.2.0 — Synthetic Software Verification

OIP v0.2.0 is a limited Python-based synthetic reference implementation. It demonstrates selected evidence-first and fail-closed authorization rules. It is not a complete implementation of the broader OIP methodology.

The documented "decide(case)" interface uses four states:

- "APPROVED"
- "PENDING_EVIDENCE"
- "BLOCKED"
- "INVALID_INPUT"

Test-record reconciliation

The initial release documentation reports 26 recorded tests passed. A later baseline-verification package reports 114 planned test records and 14 supplemental "Core_Reason" records, producing 128 exported rows.

The 128 rows must not be described as 128 unique planned tests. The relationship between the initial and subsequent test scopes remains subject to reconciliation against the primary artifacts.

Selected Audit Evidence Boundaries

- Malawi IHPS: An identifier role was approved within the documented audit; row-level linkage was not established.
- Uganda UNPS: Candidate outcomes were not automatically authorized as valid constructs.
- Ethiopia ESS4: Qualification remained blocked where required conditions were not established.
- Tanzania NPS Wave 4: Qualification was not established.
- Nigeria GHS Wave 5: Version 01 versus Version 2 identity conflict remained unresolved.
- Tanzania KHDS: Structural overlap candidates were not authorized as a final analytical cohort.
- Waymo safety-claim audit: 601 repeated analytical grains corresponded to 1,202 rows with conflicting benchmark values. Benchmark selection, deduplication, and a road-safety verdict were not authorized.

These are audit-specific findings, not evidence of universal OIP validity.

Claim Discipline

OIP distinguishes:

1. VERIFIED FINDINGS — supported by inspectable evidence within a defined verification boundary.
2. REPORTED RESULTS — recorded in reports where independent reconstruction may remain incomplete.
3. UNRESOLVED EVIDENCE — missing, contradictory, or insufficiently qualified evidence.
4. CLAIMS NOT ESTABLISHED — empirical, predictive, causal, commercial, safety, or general alignment claims not established by available evidence.

A successful software test demonstrates conformity with its encoded expectations. It does not independently establish that those expectations are scientifically valid.

Research Position

The work includes methodology specifications, dataset qualification audits, source reconciliation, synthetic software verification, and research-stage implementation artifacts.

These activities have different evidentiary scopes.

The governing rule remains:

«When required evidence is insufficient, preserve uncertainty and stop the unauthorized inference.»
